## What it does

Search any city, neighborhood, suburb, or district. Pick your detail level. MapStitch fetches and stitches real map tiles in your browser and hands you a single high-resolution PNG — ready to send to FedEx, Staples, or any large-format print shop.

You can also **combine multiple areas** into one side-by-side map. Great for printing neighboring districts, comparing zones, or building office reference maps.

<br />

## Features

- 🔍 &nbsp;**Search anything** — cities, neighborhoods, suburbs, districts worldwide
- 🗺️ &nbsp;**Five detail levels** — from city overview all the way to individual house numbers
- 🧩 &nbsp;**Combine areas** — stitch multiple places side by side into one PNG
- 🏷️ &nbsp;**Auto-labeled** — each area gets a clean label in the final output
- ⏱️ &nbsp;**Smart estimates** — real-time tile count, image size, and time before you build
- 📱 &nbsp;**Mobile friendly** — works on any device, any browser
- 🖨️ &nbsp;**Print ready** — output at poster size (4×6ft, 3×4ft, or any custom size)

<br />

## Detail levels

| Level | What you see | Best for |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Overview | Major roads and highways | Large region posters |
| All Streets ✅ | Every street labeled | General city maps |
| Fine Detail | Granular roads and paths | Dense urban areas |
| House Numbers 🏠 | Individual address numbers | Insurance, real estate, logistics |

*Estimates are dynamic — MapStitch calculates real tile count and time for your specific area before you build.*

<br />

## How to use

1. **Search** a place — city, neighborhood, suburb, or anything Nominatim knows about
2. **Add more** areas if you want a combined map
3. **Pick** your detail level
4. **Build** — tiles download and stitch automatically in your browser
5. **Save PNG** and take it to any print shop
